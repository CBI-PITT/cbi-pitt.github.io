The first is a spinning disk confocal with live cell capabilities, Ideal for organoid imaging as well as timelapse live cell. It can be used for plate or dish based assays. Its an exciting new device generously provided to the CBI by the Hillman Cancer Center. Please contact Callen and Mike for training and access
